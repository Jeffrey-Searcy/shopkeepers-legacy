Captured September 13, 2026. Implemented and applied locally after the build, independent review, and all 279 regression checks passed. See PLAN-tavern-personality.md for evidence and limits.

- Improve the shape and feel of guest thought clouds.
- Give paired conversations distinct exchanges. Guests should react to each other instead of showing duplicate conversations.
- Let the player tell a customer that their requested item is sold out.
- Make anger readable: red coloring, steam near the ears, and an upset pose. Moving in place alone does not communicate anger.
- Improve drunken behavior. Being drunk by itself is allowed and does not require ejection.
- A drunk guest interacting with an angry guest can start a fight. The broom becomes the player's way to break up trouble and eject participants.
- **Chosen fight presentation:** a cartoon dust cloud with brief glimpses of the guests.

Keep earlier business rules: inn and tavern open independently; the player decides when to close; upset guests do not reduce future arrivals. Preserve the management menu for management, not mandatory individual service.

Jeff's confirmed broom rule: eject only the targeted guest. The other participant stays and calms down. Paid lodgers keep their rooms.
