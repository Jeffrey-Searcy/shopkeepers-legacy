# Town interior furnishing atlas

Built-in image generation produced one transparent sixteen-prop atlas. The original PNG is
preserved at `public/assets/objects/town-interior-props.png`. Runtime frame crops use measured
alpha bounds; no bitmap pixels were repainted or resized. Final source is1254×1254 pixels;
the requested canvas size was advisory, so integration validates the actual source instead.

## Exact prompt

Create ONE production pixel-art sprite atlas for a cozy fantasy farming/shopkeeping game. Square canvas 1536x1536, exactly FOUR equal columns and FOUR equal rows, 16 isolated object sprites, each wholly inside its own equal cell with wide transparent padding; every cell same size, grid must be geometrically regular. REAL alpha transparent background throughout, NO checkerboard, NO colored background, NO grid lines, no labels/text, no shadows outside the objects. All objects face the bottom of screen, orthographic front elevation with a SMALL visible top plane like Stardew Valley/Travellers Rest furniture. Crisp deliberate pixel clusters, 32x40 native-pixel detail feel, broad warm wood/aged bronze/slate forms, no thin vector lines, no smooth gradients. Rich but readable high-quality furniture sprites, cozy warm material detail and dark brown outlines, not simplistic flat polygons. Never rotate sideways or isometric. Each object fully contained in its cell and separate, with clear empty margins. Row1 left to right: a wide two-level wooden bookcase packed with varied books; a museum stone pedestal displaying a small amber ancient vase; a heavy iron blacksmith anvil on a sturdy wooden stump; three ale barrels on a horizontal wooden rack with brass taps. Row2 left to right: two golden hay bales tied with rope; wooden shelving containing rolled fabric and folded colored cloth; low herbalist bench holding leafy potted herbs; alchemist worktable holding curved glass retorts and colored potion bottles. Row3 left to right: wooden fishing rack with three hanging silver fish; stack of sawn timber planks in an open wooden rack; a stone pedestal with a glowing purple crystal cluster (NO outer bloom); a freestanding polished medieval armor display. Row4 left to right: wooden tool rack with hammer/saw/tongs; a tidy coil of thick rope on a small wooden tray; a tailor's linen dressmaking mannequin on a wooden stand; a wooden coal bin with visible dark coal lumps. All 16 are game-world furnishing sprites, no people or buildings. Preserve exact 4x4 order and separation. Max object height 80% of its cell, max width 80% of its cell. These must read clearly alongside a 32-pixel-tall game character.
