# Production facade prompts

Built-in image_gen, separate image per building. No CLI fallback. New source art, not edits to rejected v2 drafts.

## market

Use case: stylized-concept. Produce ONE finished original pixel-art building facade sprite for a cozy 16-bit top-down farming game. This is a game sprite, NOT a high-resolution painting or diorama. Frontal orthographic elevation: face points exactly toward viewer, straight horizontal base and windows, some visible roof top but no rotated side face or isometric perspective. Restrained warm palette, crisp broad pixel clusters, subtle dark contour, two or three shade steps, top-left light. Truly transparent alpha outside the building silhouette, hard cutout edges, no atmospheric shadow/glow/cloud behind it. Square composition with the full building and modest transparent padding. NO TEXT or readable names, no characters, no landscape. IMPORTANT integration format: ROOF AND VERTICAL WALL ONLY. Wall ends at a single straight horizontal bottom line. No ground, steps, foundation platform, porch deck or railings, as the game draws these separately. One empty dark tall RECTANGULAR main doorway, no closed door leaf, centered on the building's main wall and touching its bottom edge. Door aperture is a simple upright dark rectangle, 24pixels wide and40pixels tall at game scale: aboutone sixth of a compact facade width and aboutone third of the wall-plus-roof height. Keep at leastone tile clear on either side of the aperture. A normal32pixel-tall game person needs to fit inside. Decorative supports stay outside the door. Show the sprite as enlarged native pixel art with few clean colors and strong shapes; no grainy microtextures or blur. Intended native structural width 160px on a16px tile grid; architecturally distinct silhouette. Subject: Tessa’s general market: asymmetrical taller attached home wing on the left, low broad shop wing right, terracotta roof, cream plaster and oak, sage-and-cream cloth awning over main entrance, produce visible in shop windows. No porch railing.
